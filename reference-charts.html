Electrical & Cooling Reference Charts

The tables people look up again and again, on pages built to be printed, downloaded and checked. Every figure is computed from a formula shown on the page.

Each chart here answers one lookup question and shows how its numbers were worked out. They sit beside the calculators: a calculator gives one answer for your inputs, a chart shows the whole range at once, which is what you want on a wall, in a truck or in a submittal.

AWG

AWG Wire Size Chart

Diameter, area in cmil and mm², and resistance per 1000 ft for 18 AWG through 1000 kcmil.

 kVA

kVA to Amps Chart

Full-load amps for standard transformer sizes at 208, 240, 480 and 600 V.

 kW

kW to Amps Chart

Current for 1 kW to 1,000 kW at 208, 240, 480 and 600 V three-phase, and 120 and 240 V single-phase.

 OCPD

Breaker Size Chart

Each standard breaker rating with the continuous load it can carry and what that is in kW.

 VD

Voltage Drop Chart

The longest run each copper wire size can make at 3% drop, for 120 V, 240 V and 480 V circuits.

 RPM

Motor Speed Chart

Synchronous speed for 2 to 12 poles at 60 and 50 Hz, with the torque each horsepower produces.

 HP

HP to kW Chart

Standard NEMA horsepower ratings in kW, and standard IEC kW ratings in horsepower.

 BTU

Cooling Load Chart

IT load in kW as BTU/h and tons, with the airflow and chilled water flow each needs.

 AMP

Wire Ampacity Chart

Allowable ampacity for copper and aluminum, 14 AWG to 500 kcmil, at 60, 75 and 90°C, with the correction factors.

 FLC

Motor Full-Load Current Chart

Full-load current by horsepower for three-phase motors at 208, 230, 460 and 575 V, and single-phase at 115 and 230 V.

Two kinds of chart

Most of the charts here are computed: every figure comes from a formula shown on the page, so any cell can be checked by hand. Two are different. The wire ampacity chart and the motor full-load current chart carry values that correspond to tables in the National Electrical Code. Those pages say so, and the figures that apply to an installation are the ones in the edition your jurisdiction has adopted, with its correction factors applied. The guides to ampacity derating and NEC 430.250 explain the method behind each.

US and metric

Four of the charts also come in metric: voltage drop, wire sizes, cooling load and motor current. Each has a US / Metric switch beside its print button, and the choice is remembered and shared with the calculators. The rest have no unit to switch, because amps, volts and kilowatts are the same in both systems.

Using and sharing these charts

Print them, download each one as a PDF or its data as CSV, and link to them freely. If you reproduce a chart, a link back to the page is appreciated. If you find a figure you can show is wrong, the contact page says how to report it.
