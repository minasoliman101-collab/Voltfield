Not professional engineering advice

Everything on Voltfield, including the calculators, guides, glossary, practice tools and reference tables, is general information for education and early-stage estimating. It is not a stamped calculation, a design, a power system study, a code interpretation or a safety procedure, and using it does not create an engineer-client relationship.

Electrical work can injure or kill and can destroy equipment. Before you rely on any figure for design, procurement, installation, commissioning or work on or near energized equipment, have it checked by a qualified person: a licensed professional engineer for design, and a qualified electrical worker following NFPA 70E or the rules that apply where you are for work on site. The adopted edition of the code in your jurisdiction, the manufacturer's documentation and the authority having jurisdiction take precedence over anything written here.

What the calculators do and do not do

Each calculator page states the method it uses, shows a worked example and lists what the method leaves out. The methods are deliberately simplified. They do not replace a short-circuit, coordination, arc-flash, load-flow or harmonic study, and they do not account for every condition on a real installation. A result that looks reasonable is not evidence that an installation is safe or compliant.

Price ranges, lead times and similar figures are indicative. They are compiled as described on the methodology page and are not quotes, offers or live inventory. Standards and code sections are summarized and paraphrased; the published standard is the authority, and editions change.
