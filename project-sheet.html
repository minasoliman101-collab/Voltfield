    What the sheet is for

    A real job is rarely one calculation. Sizing a feeder means a load current, a wire size, a voltage drop check and a breaker; a small plant room means a heat load, an airflow and a pump. The sheet keeps those results together, in the order you saved them, with the inputs that produced each one, so the working can be read by someone else or by you next week.

    Each line keeps a link that reopens its calculator with the same inputs. Change a figure there, save again, and the line is replaced with the new result.

    Where it is kept

    In this browser, on this device, and nowhere else. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, which means the sheet is private and also that it does not travel: it will not be on your phone if you built it on your laptop, and clearing your browser’s site data removes it. To keep a copy or pass it on, print it, save it as a PDF, or copy it as text.

    These are screening estimates. The sheet records what the calculators returned for the inputs shown. It is a way to keep your working together, not a design document, and it does not replace the adopted code or a calculation by a qualified engineer.
