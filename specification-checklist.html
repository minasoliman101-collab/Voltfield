Where this fits

Once your spec is solid, look the part up in the spec library to check ratings, configuration options and indicative lead time. The templates above are what you then hand to whoever builds it — Voltfield does not quote or supply anything.

Frequently asked questions

What should a complete specification for industrial or energy equipment include?

At minimum: the technical spec (voltage class, rating, standard), quantity, required delivery date and location, applicable compliance/certification requirements, budget range if you have one, and your commercial terms. Missing any of these is the most common reason responses come back inconsistent or hard to compare.

Why does every manufacturer need the exact same information?

If vendors receive different levels of detail, their quotes aren't actually comparable — one bidder might quote a cheaper unit because they weren't told about a certification requirement the others assumed. Sending an identical, complete spec to every bidder is what makes a bid comparison meaningful.

Are these templates specific to Voltfield?

No — they're generic technical specification templates you can send to any vendor, not just Voltfield. They're meant to help you solicit consistent, complete quotes across your whole bidder list.
