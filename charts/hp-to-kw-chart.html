HP to kW Chart

Standard motor ratings converted both ways: NEMA horsepower sizes in kilowatts, and IEC kilowatt sizes in horsepower.

One horsepower is 0.7457 kW, so kW is horsepower times 0.746 and horsepower is kW times 1.341. A 50 hp motor is 37.3 kW. This is output power at the shaft; the electrical input is higher, by the motor's losses.

How to read it

NEMA and IEC motors come in different standard sizes, and the two series were chosen to correspond closely: 0.75 kW is 1 hp, 7.5 kW is 10 hp, 37 kW is 50 hp, 75 kW is 100 hp.

To find the electrical power a motor draws, divide the kW shown by its efficiency. A 50 hp motor at 93 percent efficiency draws 37.3 / 0.93 = 40.1 kW at full load.

How the values are calculated

kW = hp × 0.7457
hp = kW × 1.341
Input kW = output kW / efficiency

Every figure in the chart comes from the formulas above. Nothing is copied from a published table, so any cell can be checked by hand.

What this chart does not tell you

The conversion is exact for mechanical horsepower. It gives shaft output, not electrical input, and it does not give current: full-load amps depend on voltage, efficiency and power factor, and for sizing conductors and protection the NEC requires the values in its motor tables, not a calculated figure. Metric horsepower, used on some European nameplates, is slightly smaller at 0.7355 kW.

This is a reference, not a design. It is here to give you the right order of magnitude and a figure to check against. It does not replace the adopted code or a calculation by a qualified engineer.

Frequently asked questions

How many kW is 1 hp?

One mechanical horsepower is 0.7457 kW, usually rounded to 0.746. So 10 hp is 7.46 kW and 100 hp is 74.6 kW.

Is motor horsepower the input or the output?

The output. A motor nameplate gives the mechanical power available at the shaft. The electrical power drawn from the supply is larger, by the motor's losses: output divided by efficiency.

What is the IEC equivalent of a 50 hp motor?

A 37 kW motor. 50 hp converts to 37.3 kW, and 37 kW is the standard IEC rating that corresponds to it.
