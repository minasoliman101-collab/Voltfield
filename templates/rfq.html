Request for Quotation (RFQ) Template for Engineered Equipment

A request for quotation that asks every bidder the same question and tells them exactly what to send back. Fill it in here, then print it or save it as a PDF.

A request for quotation asks suppliers to price a defined scope on stated terms by a stated date. Its real job is to make the answers comparable. If each bidder receives the same specification, the same quantities, the same delivery point and the same list of things to return, the bids can be laid side by side. If not, you will be comparing three different interpretations of what you wanted.

Printing an empty form gives you a blank template. What you type stays in this browser and is not sent anywhere.

What goes in each field

Bids due
Give a date, a time and a time zone. Allow enough time for a real answer: a manufacturer pricing a transformer to your loss factors is doing design work, and a week is not enough.
Governing specification and data sheet
The specification says what you require. The data sheet is the form each bidder fills in with what it is offering: ratings, losses, impedance, weights, dimensions. Asking every bidder to complete the same data sheet is what makes the technical comparison possible. The specification checklist covers what the specification itself should state.
Required on site
State the date you need, and ask for the lead time separately. Bidders who cannot meet the date will tell you, which is better learned now.
Incoterms rule and named place
Fix the delivery basis so that every price includes the same freight. Otherwise one bid is delivered to site and another stops at the factory gate. See the Incoterms guide.
Loss factors A and B
For transformers, state the dollar value you place on each kilowatt of no-load and load loss. Bidders then optimize the design for your cost of energy, and you compare bids on total owning cost. Leave these out and you will be offered the cheapest unit to build. The method is in how to compare equipment bids.
Scope and price schedule
List the lines you want priced separately. Anything you may want to drop or compare, such as witness testing, spares and freight, should be its own line. The price columns are for the bidder.
Each bid must include
Tick what applies and delete nothing a bidder might otherwise leave out. The exceptions list is the most important item: require it even when the answer is “none”.
How bids will be evaluated
Say it plainly. Bidders respond to what is being scored, and a stated basis protects the award from argument later.
Questions
Take questions in writing up to a cut-off date and send every answer to every bidder. An answer given to one bidder alone changes the scope for that bidder only.

Mistakes that cost money later

  • Sending bidders different versions of the specification, or answering one bidder’s question privately.
  • No delivery basis, so freight is in some prices and not in others.
  • No requirement to list exceptions, so they surface after award.
  • Asking for a price without a data sheet, which leaves nothing to check the delivered equipment against.
  • A deadline too short for a considered bid, which favors whoever had a stock design on the shelf.

Questions

What is the difference between an RFQ, an RFP and an RFI?

A request for information (RFI) gathers facts about suppliers and what is available, with no pricing commitment. A request for quotation (RFQ) asks for a price for a scope the buyer has already defined. A request for proposal (RFP) describes a need and asks suppliers to propose how they would meet it, so both the solution and the price are being compared.

Is an RFQ legally binding?

An RFQ is an invitation to quote, and issuing one does not ordinarily oblige the buyer to buy. The supplier’s quotation is normally an offer, which becomes a contract when the buyer accepts it, typically with a purchase order. How this works in a given case depends on the wording and the governing law.

How many suppliers should receive an RFQ?

Three to five qualified suppliers is typical. Fewer gives little competition; many more makes serious bidders less willing to invest in a careful quotation. Send it only to suppliers you would be prepared to award to.

What should an RFQ for a transformer include?

The specification and a data sheet for the bidder to complete, quantity, required date and delivery point with an Incoterms rule, the loss evaluation factors, the factory tests required, the commercial terms that will apply, the evaluation basis and the list of items each bid must contain.

This is a general-purpose template to help you lay a document out clearly. It is not legal advice, and it does not replace your company’s terms, your carrier’s tariff or the requirements of any regulation that applies to your shipment.
