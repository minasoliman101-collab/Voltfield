HS Code Reference (Common Equipment Categories)

The Harmonized System (HS) is the WCO's internationally standardized product classification, used as the basis for every country's customs tariff schedule. The 4-digit headings below are stable across countries; the more specific 6–10-digit subheading that actually sets your duty rate depends on technical details (voltage class, power rating, technology) and the importing country's own tariff schedule — always confirm final classification with a licensed customs broker.

External Data & Price Trackers

Copper/aluminum pricing and interconnection queue congestion both move fast enough that a static number on this page would be stale within days — so rather than publish figures we can't keep current, here are the real sources procurement and interconnection teams actually track:

  • LME Copper and LME Aluminium — London Metal Exchange official daily reference prices, the standard benchmark for copper and aluminum used in cable, bus, and transformer winding cost.
  • EIA Electricity Data — U.S. Energy Information Administration's industrial and commercial electricity price data by state, useful as an input to the transformer TCO estimator.
  • Berkeley Lab "Queued Up" — Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory's annual survey of US interconnection queues, aggregating data from ISOs/RTOs and utility balancing areas nationwide.
