Contact Voltfield

Report an error, ask how a number was worked out, or ask about citing or embedding something from the site.

How to reach us

Email: Help@voltfield.org
Phone: +1 (850) 686-1378

Email is the better route for anything that involves a number, a formula or a code reference, because it lets us reply with the working.

Reporting an error

If a calculator gives a result you can show is wrong, or a guide states something a standard does not support, we want to know. It helps to include:

  • the address of the page;
  • the inputs you used and the result you got, for a calculator;
  • what you expected, and the standard, table or source it comes from.

Corrections are made on the page itself and its last-updated date changes with them. The About page describes how corrections are handled, with an example, and the methodology page sets out how the figures on the site are compiled.

Questions about the data and methods

Each calculator page shows the formula it uses and a worked example, and each guide ends with a Scope & sources section. If something there is unclear or looks incomplete, email us and say which page.

Citing, linking and embedding

You do not need permission to link to any page or to quote from it with attribution. Every guide ends with a ready-to-paste citation. The Lead-Time Index and the voltage drop, transformer sizing and power factor calculators can be embedded on another site with the snippet given on each of those pages. For anything beyond that, ask.

What we cannot help with

Voltfield is a reference. It does not sell, quote or supply equipment, and it cannot review a specific design, size equipment for a project or give a code interpretation for your installation. Those belong with a licensed engineer and the authority having jurisdiction. The terms of use say more about what the tools are and are not for.

Privacy

A message you send is used only to answer it. See the privacy policy.
