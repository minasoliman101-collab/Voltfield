Power Generation and Grid: Equipment by Sector

How each kind of plant makes power, what its main equipment is, and what is different about specifying, buying and shipping it.

Every power plant ends the same way: a generator or an inverter, a step-up transformer, a breaker and a line to the grid. What comes before that differs a great deal, and so do the numbers that matter, the standards that apply and the things that go wrong in procurement. Each page here covers one sector in the same order: how it makes power, the main equipment and what a specification has to pin down, the figures worth knowing, and what is distinctive about buying it.
