The reference, packaged to work offline

Everything on this site stays free and stays here. These packets are the same material gathered into something you can put in a project folder, hand to a colleague, or attach to a submittal — sourced, dated, and readable without a browser tab open.

None of these are finished yet. This page exists so the plan is visible and so you can tell us which one to build first — there is nothing to buy on it, no price has been set, and nothing here is a preorder.

Which one first?

There is no point building all six before finding out whether any of them is wanted. Tell us which is closest to a problem you actually have, and we will build that one and email you when it exists. One message about that packet — not a newsletter, and not a signup for anything else.

What this is not. Voltfield does not sell equipment and is not a route to anyone who does. These packets would be documents — the reference material on this site, gathered and formatted. The site itself stays free, complete and without a signup, whether or not any of this ever ships.
