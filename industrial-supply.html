Sector overview

The sector behind every other build

Every energy-infrastructure project eventually turns into an MRO project — the fasteners, gaskets, raw stock, and safety gear that keep a plant running day to day. It's the largest single sector in the catalog by configuration count, and the one procurement teams most often under-plan for until a shutdown or turnaround is already underway.

Like every other sector on the site, Industrial Supply is sourced through Voltfield's distributor and mill network — pricing shown is indicative, drawn from that network, with more sourcing channels added as data allows.

8,106 configurations across 44 categories — industrial fasteners and hardware, raw materials, safety and PPE supplies, tools, plumbing, electrical/datacomm, packaging, and metalworking supplies for everyday plant maintenance.
