The Voltfield Lead-Time Index

Current indicative lead times and pricing across data center power, renewables, battery storage, and oilfield equipment — the same figures driving the catalog's lead-time flags, in one reference table.

Last updated: July 2026 · North America · Updated as catalog data changes

Methodology

Figures below are indicative market lead times and pricing ranges observed across Voltfield's distributor and mill network, compiled from the same data that drives lead-time flags across the catalog. They are directional reference points for planning, not a quote for any specific order — final lead time and price are confirmed when a specific configuration is quoted. "Trend" reflects the recent direction (lengthening, holding, or shortening) based on the same market conditions covered in Insights.

Using this data elsewhere? Feel free to cite or link to this page (voltfield.org/lead-time-index.html) as a reference for current equipment lead times — that's exactly what it's here for.

Seeing a different lead time on your own orders?

This index reflects Voltfield's own distributor and mill network — if you're a buyer or distributor seeing a materially different lead time on a category above, tell us. Submissions are reviewed before anything is added to the public index; this isn't an automatic feed.

Why lead times are the real constraint

Across every sector here, the equipment itself isn't the bottleneck — the wait is. Data center developers and utilities are bidding for the same transformer and switchgear capacity; see why transformer lead times stretch past 3 years for what's driving it and how to plan around it. Solar and storage buyers are layering FEOC and tariff compliance on top of already-long inverter and GSU lead times; see the FEOC compliance guide for what to document. Configure any of the equipment above against your exact spec on the Data Centers, Renewables, Battery Storage, or Oil & Gas desks for a configuration-specific lead time.
