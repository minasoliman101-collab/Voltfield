The Voltfield Lead-Time Index

Current indicative lead times and pricing across data center power, renewables, battery storage, and oilfield equipment — the same figures driving the catalog's lead-time flags, in one reference table.

Last updated: July 2026 · North America · Updated as catalog data changes

Methodology

Figures below are indicative market lead times and pricing ranges compiled from published market data, and are the same values that drive the lead-time flags across the catalog and the family reference pages. They are directional reference points for planning, not a quote for any specific order — final lead time and price come from whoever actually supplies the equipment. "Trend" reflects the recent direction (lengthening, holding, or shortening) based on the same market conditions covered in Insights.

Where each number comes from

Every row carries a basis label so anyone quoting this index can see exactly what kind of figure they're citing, rather than having to take the whole table on trust. We'd rather be specific about what's an estimate than let a modeled number get repeated as if it were a manufacturer survey.

Modeled

Derived from Voltfield's own catalog reference data and published market ranges. A Voltfield estimate — attribute it to Voltfield, not to a third party.

Operator

Reported by a buyer or distributor through the submission form below, reviewed by hand before publishing.

Published

Traceable to a named public source, linked inline on the row.

Every figure currently published is modeled. As operator submissions are verified and published sources are matched to specific rows, those rows get relabeled — so the mix here is itself a signal of how well-corroborated the index is at any given time.

Using this data elsewhere? Feel free to cite or link to this page (voltfield.org/lead-time-index.html) as a reference for current equipment lead times — that's exactly what it's here for. Please carry the basis label across with the number: a figure marked Modeled should be described as a Voltfield estimate.

How to cite this index

Writing about equipment lead times? Copy the line below. No permission needed, no attribution fee, no email required — a link back is all we ask.

Why lead times are the real constraint

Across every sector here, the equipment itself isn't the bottleneck — the wait is. Data center developers and utilities are bidding for the same transformer and switchgear capacity; see why transformer lead times stretch past 3 years for what's driving it and how to plan around it. Solar and storage buyers are layering FEOC and tariff compliance on top of already-long inverter and GSU lead times; see the FEOC compliance guide for what to document. Configure any of the equipment above against your exact spec on the Data Centers, Renewables, Battery Storage, or Oil & Gas sectors for a configuration-specific lead time.
