Utility-Scale Solar Plant Single-Line Diagram: A Typical Arrangement

A utility-scale photovoltaic plant drawn from the grid down to the modules, with each step in voltage explained.

A typical arrangement for learning. It carries no ratings on purpose and is not a design. Symbols are explained in the legend.

Reading it from the top

Point of interconnection
Where the plant meets the utility. Ownership, metering and the grid operator’s requirements all change here. See the interconnection process.
Disconnect switch and high-voltage breaker
The utility needs a visible, lockable means of isolating the plant, and the breaker clears faults on either side. Revenue metering sits at this level.
Main power transformer
Steps the collector voltage, commonly 34.5 kV, up to transmission voltage. One of the longest-lead items on the project.
Collector bus
Gathers the feeders. Each feeder is a string of pad-mounted transformers daisy-chained on underground cable.
Pad-mounted transformer
Steps the inverter’s low AC voltage up to collector voltage. See pad-mount vs. GSU transformers.
Inverter
Converts DC from the array to AC. The ratio of array DC power to inverter AC power is the DC/AC ratio.
Combiners
Parallel many strings of modules, each string fused, into the few large conductors that feed the inverter.
PV array
Modules in series form a string whose voltage must stay under the system limit on the coldest day: see the solar string calculator.
Station service
Feeds tracker motors, plant controls, communications and lighting. The plant draws this even at night.

What a real single-line adds

A single-line diagram for construction is a controlled document that an engineer is responsible for. Compared with this one it would also show:

  • Voltage, MVA and impedance for each transformer, and the number and rating of inverters per feeder
  • Protection relays and their device numbers at the main breaker and each feeder
  • Grounding method for the collector system, often a grounding transformer
  • Capacitor banks or other reactive support if the interconnection study requires them
  • The plant controller and its measurement point
  • DC-side details: string counts, combiner ratings and disconnects, which usually go on a separate DC single-line
