Obsolete & End-of-Life Reference

Discontinued equipment — and what the OEM says to replace it with

Series-level end-of-life records for the platforms our customers still run: legacy solar inverters, air and molded-case breakers, UPS, PLCs, drives, relays and instrumentation. Each record shows the manufacturer's documented migration path — or the honest answer when there isn't one — plus where Voltfield can help source legacy spares or the successor.

Verify before sourcing: these mappings are series-level and directional. Model-for-model replacements depend on frame size, ratings, firmware, and site conditions — always confirm against the OEM's current migration documentation (we'll help you verify this). Voltfield is an independent sourcing agent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the manufacturers listed.
