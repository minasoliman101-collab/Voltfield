ANSI C57

The IEEE/ANSI C57 standard series governs power transformer design, testing, and performance requirements in North America.

Definition

The IEEE/ANSI C57 standard series governs power transformer design, testing, and performance requirements in North America. It's the reference behind most of the specs on the Data Centers sector's transformer listings.

In practice

C57 is a family, not a single document. C57.12.00 sets general requirements for liquid-immersed transformers and C57.12.01 does the same for dry-type units; C57.12.90 and C57.12.91 are the test codes; C57.91 and C57.96 are the loading guides; and further parts cover bushings, tap changers, terminal markings and sound levels.

A specification that says only "to ANSI C57" has said very little. The useful ones name the part that applies and then state what the standard leaves to the purchaser: impedance, insulation level, temperature rise, cooling class, sound level and the tests to be witnessed.

Worth knowing

  • The standards are published by IEEE and approved as American National Standards, which is why both names appear.
  • Outside North America the equivalent family is IEC 60076, and the two differ in ratings, test levels and notation.
