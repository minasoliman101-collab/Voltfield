Motors: Practice Questions

Synchronous speed, slip, torque, horsepower and kW, voltage unbalance and the affinity laws.

Speed follows from frequency and poles, torque from power and speed, and the rest from those. The last question brings in the affinity laws, which decide what a variable speed drive saves on a fan or pump.

Question 1

What is the synchronous speed of a four-pole motor on a 60 Hz supply?

Choices for question 1
Show the worked solution

Answer: A. 1,800 rpm

N = 120 × f / poles = 120 × 60 / 4 = 1,800 rpm. 3,600 rpm is two poles, 1,200 rpm is six poles, and 1,500 rpm is four poles at 50 Hz.

Related: Motor Speed & Slip Calculator

Question 2

A four-pole, 60 Hz induction motor runs at 1,750 rpm at full load. What is its slip?

Choices for question 2
Show the worked solution

Answer: B. 2.78 %

Slip = (synchronous speed − actual speed) / synchronous speed = (1,800 − 1,750) / 1,800 = 2.78 percent. Dividing by the actual speed instead of the synchronous speed gives 2.86 percent.

Related: Motor Speed & Slip Calculator

Question 3

What torque does a 50 hp motor deliver at 1,775 rpm?

Choices for question 3
Show the worked solution

Answer: B. 147.9 lb-ft

T = 5,252 × hp / rpm = 5,252 × 50 / 1,775 = 147.9 lb-ft. The constant comes from the definition of horsepower, 33,000 foot-pounds per minute, divided by 2π.

Related: Motor Torque Calculator

Question 4

What is the output of a 50 hp motor in kilowatts?

Choices for question 4
Show the worked solution

Answer: C. 37.3 kW

kW = hp × 0.7457 = 50 × 0.7457 = 37.3 kW. This is shaft output. Dividing by 0.7457 converts the wrong way.

Related: HP to kW Chart

Question 5

That 50 hp motor has a full-load efficiency of 93 percent. What electrical power does it draw at full load?

Choices for question 5
Show the worked solution

Answer: B. 40.1 kW

Input = output / efficiency = 37.3 / 0.93 = 40.1 kW. The input is always larger than the output, so multiplying by the efficiency must be wrong.

Related: Motor HP to kW Calculator

Question 6

What is the synchronous speed of a six-pole motor on a 50 Hz supply?

Choices for question 6
Show the worked solution

Answer: C. 1,000 rpm

N = 120 × 50 / 6 = 1,000 rpm. The same motor on 60 Hz would have a synchronous speed of 1,200 rpm.

Related: Motor Speed Chart

Question 7

The three line-to-line voltages at a motor are 460 V, 467 V and 450 V. By the NEMA method, what is the voltage unbalance?

Choices for question 7
Show the worked solution

Answer: C. 1.96 %

Average = (460 + 467 + 450) / 3 = 459 V. The reading furthest from the average is 450 V, a deviation of 9 V. Unbalance = 9 / 459 = 1.96 percent. Using the spread between highest and lowest, 17 V, overstates it.

Related: Voltage Unbalance Calculator

Question 8

A centrifugal fan in a system with no static head is slowed to 80 percent of its speed. About what fraction of its original power does it now draw?

Choices for question 8
Show the worked solution

Answer: D. About 51 percent.

By the affinity laws, flow goes with speed, pressure with its square and power with its cube: 0.8³ = 0.512. The 64 percent figure is the pressure, and 80 percent is the flow.
