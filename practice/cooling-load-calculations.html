Cooling Load Calculations: Practice Questions

Kilowatts to BTU per hour and tons, airflow for a rack, chilled water flow, and liquid cooling flow in metric units.

Almost every watt an IT load draws leaves the room as heat, so the cooling load starts from the electrical load. The constants do the rest: 3,412 BTU per hour in a kilowatt, 12,000 BTU per hour in a ton, 1.08 for air and 500 for water in US units. Each question gives the temperature difference it needs.

Question 1

How much heat does a 50 kW IT load reject, in BTU per hour?

Choices for question 1
Show the worked solution

Answer: D. 170,607 BTU/h

BTU/h = kW × 3,412 = 50 × 3,412 = 170,607 BTU/h.

Question 2

A cooling load is 360,000 BTU per hour. How many tons is that?

Choices for question 2
Show the worked solution

Answer: B. 30 tons

One ton of cooling is 12,000 BTU per hour: 360,000 / 12,000 = 30 tons.

Question 3

A rack dissipates 5 kW, and the air passing through it rises by 25°F. What airflow does it need?

Choices for question 3
Show the worked solution

Answer: C. 632 CFM

CFM = BTU/h / (1.08 × ΔT) = 17,061 / (1.08 × 25) = 632 CFM, about 126 CFM per kW. A wider temperature rise needs less air.

Question 4

A rack dissipates 8 kW and receives 1,000 CFM of air. By how much does the air temperature rise across it?

Choices for question 4
Show the worked solution

Answer: A. 25.3 °F

ΔT = BTU/h / (1.08 × CFM) = 27,297 / (1.08 × 1,000) = 25.3°F.

Question 5

Chilled water has to carry away 500 kW of heat with a 12°F difference between supply and return. What flow is needed?

Choices for question 5
Show the worked solution

Answer: B. 284.3 GPM

BTU/h = 500 × 3,412 = 1,706,071. GPM = BTU/h / (500 × ΔT) = 1,706,071 / (500 × 12) = 284.3 GPM.

Question 6

A chiller is rated 150 tons. What is that in kilowatts of cooling?

Choices for question 6
Show the worked solution

Answer: A. 527.5 kW

One ton of cooling is 3.517 kW: 150 × 3.517 = 527.5 kW. This is the heat the chiller removes, not the electricity it uses.

Question 7

A liquid-cooled rack rejects 80 kW to water, which warms by 10 K as it passes through. What flow is needed, in litres per minute?

Choices for question 7
Show the worked solution

Answer: B. 114.7 L/min

Mass flow = kW / (4.186 × ΔT) = 80 / (4.186 × 10) = 1.911 kg/s. Water is close to one kilogram per litre, so that is 1.911 L/s, or 114.7 L/min.

Question 8

A cooling load of 900 tons is served by chillers of 300 tons each. How many chillers does an N+1 design install?

Choices for question 8
Show the worked solution

Answer: B. 4

N is the number needed to carry the load: 900 / 300 = 3. N+1 adds one spare, so four chillers are installed, and any one can be out of service.
