A facility uses 200,000 kWh in a month with a peak demand of 500 kW. Energy costs $0.08 per kWh and demand costs $15 per kW. What is the bill, before taxes and fixed charges?

Question 2 of 8 in the set on energy, demand and efficiency. Choose an answer, then open the solution to see the working.

Choose an answer

A facility uses 200,000 kWh in a month with a peak demand of 500 kW. Energy costs $0.08 per kWh and demand costs $15 per kW. What is the bill, before taxes and fixed charges?
Show the answer and worked solution

Answer: C. $23,500

Energy charge = 200,000 × 0.08 = $16,000. Demand charge = 500 × 15 = $7,500. Total = $23,500. The demand charge is nearly a third of the bill, and it is set by the single highest interval in the month.

Why the other choices are wrong

  • A. $7,500 $7,500 is the demand charge alone.
  • B. $16,000 $16,000 is the energy charge alone, with no demand charge.
  • D. $16,015 $16,015 adds the demand rate once, without multiplying it by the 500 kW of demand.
