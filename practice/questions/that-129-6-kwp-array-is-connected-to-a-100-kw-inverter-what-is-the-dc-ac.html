That 129.6 kWp array is connected to a 100 kW inverter. What is the DC/AC ratio?
Show the answer and worked solution

Answer: D. 1.30

DC/AC ratio = array DC capacity / inverter AC rating = 129.6 / 100 = 1.30. Dividing the other way gives 0.77, the inverse.

Why the other choices are wrong

  • A. 1.03 Has no basis in the two figures given.
  • B. 29.60 This is the difference between the two figures in kilowatts, not their ratio.
  • C. 0.77 This is the ratio upside down.
