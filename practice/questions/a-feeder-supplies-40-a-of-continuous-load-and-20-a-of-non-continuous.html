A feeder supplies 40 A of continuous load and 20 A of non-continuous load. What is the smallest standard breaker?
Show the answer and worked solution

Answer: D. 70 A

Minimum rating = (40 × 1.25) + 20 = 70 A, which is itself a standard rating: 70 A. Only the continuous part takes the 125 percent factor.

Why the other choices are wrong

  • A. 80 A 80 A comes from applying 125 percent to the whole load. Only the continuous part takes the factor.
  • B. 60 A 60 A covers the load at 100 percent only, with nothing added for the continuous part.
  • C. 90 A 90 A is larger than needed.
