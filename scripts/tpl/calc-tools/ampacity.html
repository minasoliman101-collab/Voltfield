Ampacity Lookup

Base ampacity only — apply NEC 310.15(B) ambient/altitude correction and 310.15(C) bundling adjustment factors for your actual install conditions, and respect the 60°C/75°C termination rating of the equipment per NEC 110.14(C).

Conduit Fill

EMT conduit and THHN/THWN-2 conductor areas, a handful of common sizes only. Mixed conductor sizes in one raceway, or other conduit/conductor types, need NEC Annex C or a real fill calc.
NEC Annex C publishes the code's own pre-computed conductors-per-conduit tables, which can differ by about ±1 conductor from the simple area-ratio math here due to NFPA's own rounding conventions — use Annex C (or a licensed designer) for anything past a rough early estimate, and always confirm against the NEC/NFPA 70 edition actually adopted by your jurisdiction.
