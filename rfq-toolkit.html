Frequently asked questions

What should I include in an RFQ for industrial or energy equipment?

At minimum: the technical spec (voltage class, rating, standard), quantity, required delivery date and location, applicable compliance/certification requirements, budget range if you have one, and your commercial terms. Missing any of these is the most common reason vendor quotes come back inconsistent or hard to compare.

Why does every vendor need the exact same information in an RFQ?

If vendors receive different levels of detail, their quotes aren't actually comparable — one bidder might quote a cheaper unit because they weren't told about a certification requirement the others assumed. Sending an identical, complete spec to every bidder is what makes a bid comparison meaningful.

Are these RFQ templates specific to Voltfield?

No — they're generic technical specification templates you can send to any vendor, not just Voltfield. They're meant to help you solicit consistent, complete quotes across your whole bidder list.
