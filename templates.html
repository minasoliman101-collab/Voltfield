Procurement and Logistics Templates

The documents that carry a piece of equipment from order to site, as forms you can fill in here and print. Free, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.

Buying a transformer, a generator or a battery container leaves a paper trail, and each document in it settles a different argument. The request for quotation settles what was asked. The bid tabulation settles why one bidder was chosen. The purchase order settles what was agreed. The bill of lading settles what the carrier took. The proof of delivery settles what arrived and in what state. Between them sit the working documents: the inspection and test plan, the expediting log, the packing list and the vendor scorecard. These templates follow that order, and each page explains what every field is for and where the common mistakes are.

Before the order: asking and comparing

RFQ

Request for quotation template

One scope, one deadline, and a list of what each bidder must send back, so the bids that arrive can be compared.

 TAB

Bid tabulation template

Three bidders side by side: leveled price lines that total themselves, loss evaluation, lead time, exceptions and the recommendation.

Placing and running the order

PO

Purchase order template

Line items with totals, Incoterms, and the equipment terms a generic PO leaves out: approval drawings, factory tests, documentation.

 EXP

Expediting log template

Twelve milestones from order acknowledgment to final documents, with planned, forecast and actual dates, and a record of who promised what.

 ITP

Inspection and test plan template

Every inspection and test in sequence, with its acceptance criteria and who holds, witnesses or reviews it.

 SC

Vendor scorecard template

Seven weighted measures taken from dates and documents, with a total out of 5 that adds itself.

Shipping and receiving

BOL

Bill of lading template

Straight BOL for truck freight: shipper, consignee, carrier, freight terms and commodity lines with weight totals.

 POD

Proof of delivery template

Delivery receipt with a receiving inspection: counts against the BOL, damage, impact recorders, and the exceptions line that protects a claim.

 PL

Packing list template

Each package with contents, dimensions and weights that total themselves, plus handling and storage instructions.

For the engineering side of the order

Specification checklist

What a complete equipment specification has to state, with copy-ready sheets for transformers, switchgear and BESS.

Project sheet

Collect calculator results for one job, with their inputs, and print them as a single record.

Formula reference

The formulas behind the numbers on a data sheet, each with its variables and a calculator.

Guides that go with them

  • FAT vs. SAT: factory and site acceptance testing, witness points and punch lists.

How the templates work

  • Fill in the browser. Each template is the document itself, with the fields live. Totals add up as you type.
  • Print or save as PDF. The print button gives the document on its own, without the site around it. Print it empty for a blank form.
  • Private. What you type is kept in your browser on this device so a reload does not lose it. It is not sent to Voltfield or anyone else.
  • General, not legal. These lay a document out clearly. They do not replace your company’s terms and conditions or a carrier’s tariff.
